## 💡 Pro Tips

### 1. **Test on Real Devices**

Animations that look good on desktop may lag on mobile. Always test on:

- iPhone (Safari)
- Android (Chrome)
- Older devices (performance check)

### 2. **Use Browser DevTools**

- Performance tab to check for jank
- Network tab to check image sizes
- Lighthouse for overall score

### 3. **Gradual Rollout**

Don't implement everything at once:

1. Start with quick wins
2. Measure impact
3. Add more based on results

### 4. **User Feedback is Key**

Ask users:

- Do animations feel too slow/fast?
- Is the app responsive enough?
- Any laggy interactions?
